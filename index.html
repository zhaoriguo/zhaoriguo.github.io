Biography

I am currently a Lecturer in the Department of Artificial Intelligence, School of Electrical Engineering and Automation, Xiamen University of Technology. My research interests include large-language-model-based medical decision-making, graph-neural-network-based bioinformatics identification, multi-agent decision-making, and interactive reinforcement learning.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Southampton in 2023, M.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence from the University of Southampton in 2020, Master of Management in Business Administration from Dalian University of Technology in 2021, and B.Sc. in Geographic Information Systems from Liaoning Normal University in 2014.

Student Supervision and Teaching

I welcome undergraduate and master's students who are interested in large language models, medical AI, graph neural networks, bioinformatics identification, multi-agent systems, and interactive reinforcement learning to contact me for research discussion and collaboration.

Courses taught include: Natural Language Processing and Knowledge Representation, Game Theory and Reinforcement Learning, and Multi-Agent and Human-Machine Hybrid Intelligence.
